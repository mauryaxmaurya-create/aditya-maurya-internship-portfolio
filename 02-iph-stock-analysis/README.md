# IPH Limited FY2026 Stock Analysis

## Overview

This project analyses 254 trading days of IPH Limited data from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. The work combines an auditable Excel model with a written investment case covering price movement, trading volume, dividends, portfolio outcomes, technical indicators and risk.

## Analysis performed

- Data audit and trading-day checks
- Closing-price, candlestick and volume visualisations
- Market-capitalisation and shares-outstanding analysis
- Dividend-yield and price-to-earnings snapshots
- Whole-share buy-and-hold portfolio calculation
- Chronological single-trade and constrained multi-trade scenarios
- 20-day and 50-day moving averages
- Annualised daily-return volatility and maximum drawdown

## Key findings

- A $1,000 buy-and-hold position ended at $921.16 after dividends, a total return of -7.884%.
- The historical best single trade bought on 12 March 2026 at $3.19 and sold on 11 June 2026 at $4.24, producing a 32.9% return before costs.
- Annualised daily-return volatility was 36.6%, and maximum drawdown reached -43.3%.
- A sustained 20-day/50-day moving-average crossover occurred on 27 April 2026, providing broader support for the later recovery.

The analysis did not support an unconditional buy decision. It found a possible case for a small staged position only for an income- or value-oriented investor willing to tolerate substantial volatility and wait for price, trend and operating confirmation.

## Important limitation

This is an educational analysis of historical supplied data, not personal financial advice. Historical best-trade scenarios use hindsight and should not be interpreted as achievable future returns.
